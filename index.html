Built for size,
not for screenshots

Most venues look the same until you send a real order. These are the parts that decide what your fill looks like.

  • Sub-millisecond matching Median internal latency of 180 microseconds, measured at the gateway and published monthly.
  • Colocation Cross-connect in Equinix LD4 and NY4, with a dedicated FIX session per strategy.
  • Honest rejects No last look. An order that passes risk checks is filled or rests on the book.
  • Settlement you can plan around Fiat out by 16:00 London on the same day, for instructions received before 14:00.

const ws = new WebSocket("wss://stream.solen.markets/v1");

ws.onopen = () => ws.send(JSON.stringify({
  op: "subscribe",
  channel: "book",
  symbols: ["BTC-USD", "ETH-USD"],
  depth: 10
}));

ws.onmessage = (e) => {
  const { symbol, bids, asks } = JSON.parse(e.data);
  console.log(symbol, bids[0], asks[0]);

  size: "50"

Boring where it counts

Client assets are not a funding source. They sit apart from the company balance sheet and are reported monthly.

  • Segregated accounts. Client funds are held separately from operating capital and never lent out.
  • Proof of reserves. Monthly attestation by an independent auditor, published with the underlying addresses.
  • Withdrawal controls. Address allowlists, quorum approval and a configurable delay on first use.
  • Key custody. Hardware security modules in two jurisdictions, with no single person able to sign.
  • Independent testing. Annual penetration test and a public bug bounty with no payout cap.
  • Incident reporting. Status page, root cause write-ups and a named contact for every client.
